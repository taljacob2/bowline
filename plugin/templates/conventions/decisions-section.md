## Decisions, and questions still open

A settled design question gets an entry here in the same round it settles, without being asked: the decision and the
reasoning behind it, so a later reader finds the why and not only the what. A question that is open stays here too,
marked open, until it closes. An entry is never deleted; a reversed decision gets a new entry that says what changed
and why.

- **<The decision in a few words> (<date>).** <What was decided, in one or two sentences.> <Why: the constraint or the
  harm it avoids, in the words the question was asked in.> <What was considered and not chosen, in one sentence, when
  that helps a later reader.>
- **Open: <the question in a few words> (<date raised>).** <What is unclear and what would settle it.>
