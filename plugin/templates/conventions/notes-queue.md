# Notes queue

Feedback that arrives while a round is in progress goes here, verbatim, with its date and number, and is worked in
order once the current round is committed. A note is a question until it is answered, a request until it is done or
declined with a reason, and it leaves the queue when it is finished; what it decided goes where decisions live. The
queue is the only place a mid-work note is kept, so nothing is lost and nothing interrupts the thread.
